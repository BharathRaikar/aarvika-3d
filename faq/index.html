Questions

Before you order

How long until it arrives? +

We print after you order, so allow 3–5 working days to make it, plus shipping. Larger pieces like the hexagon wall art take longer — we'll tell you the real number over Instagram DM before you pay.

What colours are available? +

Ask us on Instagram DM before you pay. We're still confirming what we can keep in stock versus what needs a couple of extra days to order in, so we'd rather tell you straight than promise a colour we can't get.

Is it strong? Will it survive a Bengaluru summer? +

PLA is rigid and holds up fine indoors. It does soften above roughly 55°C, so don't leave a piece on a car dashboard in direct sun. That's the one real limitation.

Can I use the bowl and dish for food? +

No. 3D printed surfaces have fine layer grooves that trap bacteria, and they aren't dishwasher-safe. Treat them as decorative.

Do you take returns? +

Each piece is made for your order, so we don't take general returns. If it arrives damaged or wrong, send us a photo and we'll reprint it.

Can you print something from my own file? +

Yes — send an STL or 3MF over Instagram DM and we'll quote. We'll tell you honestly if a model won't print well before taking your money.
